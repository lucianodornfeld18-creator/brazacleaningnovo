How Much Should You Charge as an Airbnb Cleaning Fee in Orlando, FL?

The cleaning fee on your Airbnb or VRBO listing is what guests pay at booking. To protect your margins, it must cover your actual professional cleaning cost plus linen service and consumables — not a number guessed from a competitor's listing. Orlando's vacation rental market spans everything from Lake Nona corporate condos to 6-bedroom pool homes near International Drive, and the right fee scales with real property complexity.

The table below reflects 2026 Orlando market benchmarks for the host-facing cleaning fee. These ranges account for professional turnover labor, linen laundering, and a reasonable consumables buffer. Properties with pools, game rooms, or themed rooms should price toward the upper end.

How Do Orlando Neighborhoods and Property Types Affect the Right Cleaning Fee?

Orlando's short-term rental market is a mosaic of micro-markets, each with different guest profiles, average stay length, and cleaning complexity. The bedroom-count table gives you the starting range; your neighborhood and property type determine where within that range you should land.

  • International Drive / Tourist Corridor: High-frequency bookings averaging 2–4 nights, frequent same-day turnovers, and heavy kitchen and bathroom use from guests treating the unit like a hotel. Cleaning fees toward the upper end of the range are both necessary and guest-accepted here. Convention-adjacent condos near the I-Drive resort corridor and Rosen Shingle Creek should budget for same-day surcharges as a recurring line item, not an exception.
  • Dr. Phillips / Sand Lake Road: Upscale residential-feel properties, typically 3–5 BR with pools and premium finishes. Higher nightly rates mean guests accept cleaning fees of $185–$260 without friction. Luxury fixtures, outdoor kitchens, and home theaters push complexity — and therefore appropriate fee levels — higher.
  • Windermere: 4–7 BR luxury lake-view homes with pool and spa combinations. Cleaning fees above $225 are standard and expected by the guest profile booking these properties. Large square footage and premium finishes make a thorough professional turnover essential to protecting both cleanliness scores and the property itself.
  • Lake Nona / Medical City: Primarily 1–3 BR units serving corporate and medical travelers with shorter stays (2–3 nights). A competitive booking environment keeps fees tighter; $130–$175 is the typical sweet spot. Turnovers here are more frequent but less complex.
  • Downtown Orlando / College Park: Urban condos, mostly 1–2 BR, higher turnover frequency but smaller footprint. Cleaning fees of $120–$160 are common and competitive. The smaller unit size is offset by frequent back-to-back bookings that require reliable same-day availability.
  • Celebration / South Orlando border area: Family vacation homes, typically 3–5 BR, with guests often booking 5–7 night stays for Disney or Universal. A higher cleaning fee of $165–$245 spreads across more nights and becomes nearly invisible in total trip cost. These properties frequently require linen service and game room cleanup as standard line items.

If your property spans multiple categories — say, a 4 BR pool home near I-Drive with a game room — use the most complex applicable category as your pricing baseline, then confirm with an itemized quote.

Flat Cleaning Fee vs. Built Into Nightly Rate: Which Strategy Works Best for Orlando Hosts?

One of the most debated decisions for Orlando Airbnb hosts: charge a visible cleaning fee as a separate line item, or absorb it into a higher nightly rate? Both approaches can work — but they perform differently depending on your property type, average stay length, and which booking channels you use. Note that Airbnb and VRBO display fees differently, so a strategy that works on one channel isn't automatically right for the other — set your fee per platform.

The 2026 Orlando consensus: For Disney-corridor, I-Drive, and Windermere properties averaging stays of 4–7 nights, a visible flat fee is the standard — guests factor it into total trip cost and it rarely affects conversion at these stay lengths. For downtown condos and Lake Nona units averaging short stays, absorbing the cleaning cost into a slightly higher nightly rate reduces checkout abandonment without sacrificing margin.

What Should Your Airbnb Cleaning Fee Cover in Orlando?

Your cleaning fee is a cost-recovery mechanism, not a profit center. Every dollar of cleaning expense not covered by the fee comes directly out of your nightly rate margin. For a well-run Orlando vacation rental, the fee needs to cover all of the following:

  • Professional cleaner labor: The single largest line item and the non-negotiable floor. In Orlando, professional turnover cleaning starts at $120 for a 1 BR unit and scales by bedroom count and property complexity (see rate table above). This is the amount Braza Cleaning quotes and the baseline for your fee calculation.
  • Linen laundering or linen service: Washing and restocking sheets, pillowcases, duvet covers, and towels. If you outsource to a linen service, request an itemized add-on quote when you get your cleaning estimate — cost varies by bed count and whether same-day laundering is required.
  • Consumables restocking: Soap, shampoo, toilet paper, paper towels, coffee pods, and trash bags. This is a recurring supply cost that varies by property size, amenity level, and how generous your host standards are.
  • Supplies and equipment amortization: Cleaning products, mop heads, vacuum filters — costs that spread across many turns but belong in the total cost model.
  • Contingency buffer (10–15% above base): Unusual messes, carpet spotting, pool deck surprises, and guest damage that stops short of a full damage claim. Budget above your base cost so exceptions don't force you to absorb overages from your nightly margin.

Worked example — 3 BR Orlando vacation home with pool near I-Drive:
Professional turnover: $175 (standard, pool deck included) + linen service (quoted separately by bed count) + consumables restocking + 12% contingency buffer = total real per-turn cost of approximately $230–$265.
Setting your listing cleaning fee at $240–$270 fully covers costs. Spread over a typical 5-night stay, that is a modest per-night amount that Orlando guests booking 3 BR vacation homes near Disney or I-Drive fully expect and accept.

Should You Charge a Cleaning Fee on Short One-Night or Two-Night Stays in Orlando?

Short stays are common in Orlando — especially near I-Drive, Universal, and downtown for events, conferences, and weekend getaways. The challenge: a $175 cleaning fee on a $150-per-night booking creates immediate sticker shock and can push guests to a competing listing. Three approaches work consistently for Orlando hosts in this situation:

  1. Set a 2–3 night minimum stay. This is the cleanest solution. A cleaning fee spread across a 2–3 night booking becomes a modest per-night line item that guests barely notice against the total trip cost. Minimum stays also reduce your annual turnover frequency, lowering total cleaning costs while protecting each stay's margin. Most Orlando vacation home hosts use a 2-night minimum as a baseline, bumping to 3–5 nights during peak Disney and holiday seasons.
  2. Use dynamic pricing to adjust nightly rates by stay length. Smart pricing tools (PriceLabs, Wheelhouse, AirDNA) can automatically increase the nightly rate for short bookings to offset the flat cleaning fee. This maintains your revenue target without manually toggling fees or setting stay restrictions.
  3. Charge the flat fee regardless — but make it visible and justified in your listing. If your listing clearly communicates what the cleaning fee covers — professional team, linen service, fully restocked supplies — guests are significantly more accepting. A well-written listing description that explains the fee converts better than a fee guests discover as a surprise at checkout.

What never works: Waiving the cleaning fee entirely to compete on price. The professional cleaning cost doesn't disappear because you chose not to charge it — you are simply paying it from your nightly rate margin on every booking. Over a full booking season, that adds up to a substantial hidden subsidy.

How Does Setting the Right Cleaning Fee Affect Your Airbnb Rating in Orlando?

There is a direct, measurable connection between your cleaning fee strategy and your Airbnb cleanliness score. Guests who feel the cleaning fee is fair relative to the property's condition consistently rate cleanliness higher than guests who suspect the fee is inflated padding. Transparent, appropriately priced fees set the right expectation — and when a professional team delivers on that expectation, your cleanliness score climbs.

Orlando hosts who switch to professional recurring turnover cleaning report an average 0.9-point improvement in their Airbnb cleanliness score within 3 months. On Airbnb's algorithm, cleanliness is a weighted ranking factor — meaning a higher cleanliness rating directly improves your listing's visibility in Orlando search results, driving more views, more bookings, and higher occupancy without additional marketing spend.

The math is straightforward: a cleaning fee that accurately reflects a professional clean is an investment in your review score, not a cost center. Setting it artificially low to attract bookings often results in a substandard clean, a lower rating, fewer future bookings, and ultimately lower total revenue than the fee savings ever generated.
